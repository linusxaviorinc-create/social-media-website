# Approval Rules

Default policy until you change it:

- Drafting is allowed without approval.
- Public posts require approval before publish.
- Public replies require approval before send.
- Direct messages require approval before send.
- Follows, likes, and other engagement actions require approval.

## Low-risk actions Codex may take

- Organize files
- Prepare captions
- Queue drafts for review
- Save browser sessions after you log in

## Actions that require approval

- Publishing any post
- Sending any reply
- Sending any DM
- Deleting content
- Updating profile settings
