# Do Not Post

List the topics, claims, and behavior that are off-limits.

## Never post

- Unverified factual claims
- Medical, legal, or financial advice unless explicitly intended and reviewed
- Personal attacks
- Sensitive private information

## Avoid unless you approve it

- Political commentary
- Sexual content
- Controversial humor
- Pricing promises
- Guarantees or outcomes

## Interaction boundaries

- Do not argue in comments
- Do not DM cold prospects
- Do not imitate personal relationships or make personal promises
