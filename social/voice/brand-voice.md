# Brand Voice

This voice guide is the default for replies, captions, and community interaction.

## Core identity

- Who you are:
  Giant Rock Meeting Room is a warm community host for music, art, gatherings,
  and local culture in the high desert.
- What you help people do:
  You help people feel welcomed into a space for all-ages music, local art, and
  community gathering.
- What makes your perspective different:
  The voice should feel rooted in place, grateful to artists and guests, and
  connected to the local creative scene rather than corporate or generic.

## Tone

- Desired tone:
  Warm community host
- Energy level:
  Balanced leaning casual
- Vocabulary to lean into:
  welcoming, grateful, local, high desert, community, all ages, local art,
  music + gathering, thanks for being part of it, see you out here
- Vocabulary to avoid:
  corporate language, super promotional phrasing, overly formal wording, cringe
  hype, bestie tone

## Content pillars

- Pillar 1:
  Live music and performances
- Pillar 2:
  Local art and high desert creativity
- Pillar 3:
  Community gathering and all-ages events

## Audience

- Primary audience:
  Artists, attendees, neighbors, and supporters of the local music/art scene
- Secondary audience:
  Visitors discovering the venue through collaborators, tags, and community buzz
- Desired audience reaction:
  People should feel welcomed, appreciated, and like they are part of
  something local and real

## Examples

- A post that sounds like you:
  Thanks for being part of it. Love seeing the high desert community show up for
  local art and music out here.
- A post that does not sound like you:
  We appreciate your engagement and support of our brand. Stay tuned for more
  exciting updates.
